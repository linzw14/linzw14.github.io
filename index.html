Our mission

Decode life. Build with it.

The Lin Lab works at the convergence of intelligent systems and living systems. We develop technologies that can observe biology continuously, connect cellular function to molecular identity, and turn complex data into scientific action.

Our work integrates tissue-like bioelectronics, spatial transcriptomics, and agentic AI. The long-term goal is to reveal how cells organize functional tissues—and to create new ways to study development, disease, and regeneration across biological scales.

What we do

Research directions

01

Agentic AI for science

Autonomous, interpretable agents that transform complex biological analysis into reproducible scientific workflows.

02

Bioelectronic interfaces

Soft, tissue-like electronics that chronically record cellular activity without disrupting development.

03

Spatial & multimodal biology

Methods that connect fast functional dynamics with molecular identity in the very same cells.
